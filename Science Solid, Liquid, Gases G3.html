🧪 LESSON 2: PROPERTIES OF SOLIDS & LIQUIDS

Let’s Investigate: Shape & Flow Lab! 🫗

Aim: To find out if solids and liquids have a fixed shape and whether they can flow!

🔬 What Do You Observe?

Ice cubes keep their cube shape! They stack on top of one another to form a heap, leaving gaps in the beaker. Solids cannot flow!

🧊 Solid Conclusion:

  • • The shape of a solid is fixed.
  • • Solids cannot flow when poured.
  • • Solids stack on top of each other to form a heap with gaps!

💧 Liquid Conclusion:

  • • The shape of a liquid changes to fit the container.
  • • Liquids can flow when poured.
  • • When tilted, a liquid’s surface always stays flat (horizontal)!
⚠️ WATCH OUT! TRICKY QUESTION ALERT!

Are Soft Bread & Marshmallows Solids?! 🍞🍡

Some solids like glass bowls and wooden boards feel hard. Other solids like bread and marshmallows feel soft!

💡 Golden Rule: Solids, even soft ones, keep their shape if we do not apply force to them, and they cannot flow! We do NOT tell if an object is a solid by feeling whether it is hard or soft!
🍞 ➔ 🥣

Ada placed a loaf of bread into three different bowls. Did the bread flow or change into a bowl shape?

NO! Bread cannot flow and keeps its loaf shape — so Bread is a SOLID! ✅

💉 LESSON 3: VOLUME & COMPRESSION

The Syringe Squeeze Challenge! 💪

Volume is the amount of space an object occupies. Can you squeeze (compress) Ice or Water inside a closed syringe? Try pushing the plunger!

EXPERIMENT A: SOLID

Syringe Filled with Ice 🧊

Water inside this syringe was frozen into solid ice. Push the plunger!

Click the button to try compressing the ice!

EXPERIMENT B: LIQUID

Syringe Filled with Water 💧

Cover the tip with your finger so water can't escape. Push the plunger!

Click the button to try compressing the water!

🎉 Scientific Discovery: Fixed Volume!

Solids and liquids cannot be compressed! We cannot reduce the space occupied by solids or liquids — so both solids and liquids have a fixed volume!

🕵️ LESSON 4: CONSOLIDATION MIND MAP

Crack the Case: Matter Summary!

Review how Solids, Liquids, and Gases compare before taking the Final Quiz!

🌟 MATTER (Can be...)

1. SOLIDS

🧊
  • ✅ Have a fixed shape
  • ❌ Cannot flow
  • ❌ Cannot be compressed
  • ✅ Have a fixed volume

2. LIQUIDS

💧
  • 🔄 Do not have a fixed shape (take container's shape)
  • ✅ Can flow
  • ❌ Cannot be compressed
  • ✅ Have a fixed volume

3. GASES

💨
  • 🔄 Do not have a fixed shape
  • 🎈 Take the shape of the container
  • ✅ Can flow
  • 💡 Examples: Steam, Wind, Air bubbles!
